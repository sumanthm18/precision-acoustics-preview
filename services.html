Hearing assessments

Adults and children from age three

A comprehensive diagnostic hearing assessment in a quiet room. You get the results on the day, fully explained in plain English, along with what they mean for everyday listening.

Call (03) 9894 5433 to book

Hearing aid fitting

Independent advice

First, whether hearing aids will help you at all. Then which type suits your hearing and the way you live, followed by careful fitting and fine-tuning. The practice is not owned by a manufacturer, so the recommendation is about you.

Call (03) 9894 5433 to book

Hearing aid review and problem solving

For hearing aids fitted anywhere

Hearing aids that whistle, sound tinny, struggle in restaurants or spend their life in a drawer. We review the fitting, adjust what can be adjusted and give you an honest second opinion on the rest.

Call (03) 9894 5433 to book a review

Hearing aid repairs

Servicing and repairs

Hearing aids need looking after. Call and describe the problem, and we will tell you whether it needs a visit and what to bring.

Call (03) 9894 5433

Wax removal

A specialty of the practice

Safe, professional removal of ear wax, carried out by an audiologist.

Call (03) 9894 5433 to book

Custom earplugs

Made from an impression of your ear

Earplugs moulded to fit you, for noise, swimming, sleeping beside a snoring partner, hyperacusis (when everyday sounds feel uncomfortably loud) and other needs.

Call (03) 9894 5433 to enquire

Noise-induced hearing loss

Assessment of suspected noise damage

If you think noise at work or elsewhere has affected your hearing, an assessment will tell you where you stand. The practice has many years of experience in this area.
